# Groner Holz Community Forest Management Plan

Collaborative MSc forestry project completed at the University of
Göttingen in 2021.

[View the forest management plan (PDF)](groner-holz-management-plan.pdf)

## Project overview

This management plan was developed for Groner Holz Community Forest
as part of the MSc Tropical and International Forestry program.

The report connects forest resource assessments with management
objectives, recommended activities, implementation arrangements,
and financial considerations.


## What the report covers

- Forest inventory, stand stocking, and diameter distributions
- Timber volume, standing timber quality, and carbon stock estimates
- Regeneration and browsing assessments
- Skid-trail conditions and soil disturbance
- Silvicultural and forest management recommendations
- Wildlife and nature conservation objectives
- Standing timber valuation and discounted cash flow analysis
- Implementation, monitoring, and evaluation

## Project context

This report was prepared as an academic group project.
Its recommendations reflect the information and conditions
considered at the time of preparation.
